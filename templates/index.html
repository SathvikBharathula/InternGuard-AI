💡 How to Stay Safe

Research the company independently before applying or sharing personal info.
Legitimate employers never ask for upfront payments or training fees.
Be sceptical of vague roles with unusually high pay or "no experience needed".
Verify the job on the official company website or LinkedIn before proceeding.
Never wire money or send gift cards as part of a "job offer".
